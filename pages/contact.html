Frequently Asked Questions

If you have any questions, the following information may be helpful to you

Q: How do I choose the right service model?

A: You can choose based on your needs and current situation. If you have a product but lack operational capability, you can choose managed operations. If you need to develop a product from scratch, you can choose outsourced development. If you want to invest in an existing mature product, you can choose proprietary project operations. Our team can also help evaluate and select the best option for you.

Q: How long does a project typically take?

A: This depends on the complexity and scale of the project. Simple application development usually takes 3-6 months, while complex game development may take 6-12 months or longer. We will conduct a detailed assessment of your needs in the initial phase and provide you with a specific timeline.

Q: How is project cost evaluated?

A: We conduct a detailed assessment based on your specific project requirements, including functional complexity, technical requirements, and team involvement. Initial consultation is completely free, and we will provide you with professional cost assessment and return on investment forecasts.

Q: Can projects be completed on schedule?

A: We take project delivery very seriously. When reaching a cooperation agreement with clients, we clearly specify the agreed-upon delivery timeline. Our experienced project management team ensures projects are completed on time and with quality. If unforeseen circumstances occur, we will communicate promptly and adjust the plan.

Q: Is remote collaboration supported?

A: Absolutely. We have team members in various locations worldwide and are experienced with remote collaboration. Through regular video conferences, document sharing, and project management tools, we ensure smooth communication with clients and transparency in project progress.

Q: Is long-term support provided after project completion?

A: Yes. We provide free technical support and maintenance for a certain period. After that, you can choose a paid long-term support plan, including regular updates, bug fixes, and feature enhancements to ensure your product runs stably for the long term.
